Formats in this app

  • OCI: square; 200–900 px; JPEG/JPG ≤200 KB; plain light background that is not white; OCI says about 80% face coverage.
  • U.S. passport / visa: 2×2 in; head 50–69% of image height. This app exports 600×600 JPG. For online U.S. passport renewal, the State Department can crop the original photo during upload.
  • Schengen visa: common 35×45 mm framing, based on ICAO/Schengen guidance; face about 70–80% of image height. Individual consulates may have their own upload-size rules.

The guides help with framing and basic quality checks, but they cannot guarantee acceptance by an issuing authority.
