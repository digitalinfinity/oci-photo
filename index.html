OCI photo requirements

  • Square JPEG/JPG.
  • 200×200 px minimum; 900×900 px maximum.
  • Maximum file size 200 KB.
  • Plain light-colored background, not white.
  • Front view of head and shoulders; face centered.
  • Eyes open; no distracting shadows.
  • Face roughly 50–70% of photo height.

This helper checks file properties and gives framing/background guidance; it cannot guarantee OCI acceptance.
